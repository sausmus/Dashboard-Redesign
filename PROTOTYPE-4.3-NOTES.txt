TEACHER DASHBOARD REDESIGN — PROTOTYPE 4.3
STUDENTS POLISH PASS

Changes
-------
1. Sort controls
   • Roster, Participation, and Timeliness sorters now share one rounded, roomier visual style.
   • Sorters use a custom subtle chevron rather than the square browser-default appearance.
   • Participation and Timeliness headers have more spacing between the sorter, settings gear, and Full Tracker action.
   • Controls wrap cleanly on narrower screens.

2. Utility settings buttons
   • Participation and Timeliness settings gears now use the same rounded control geometry as the rest of Students.
   • No settings behavior or stored preferences changed.

3. Student Selector remaining pool
   • "Students remaining" is now an interactive, persistent count.
   • Clicking it opens an anchored informational popover.
   • Remaining students are alphabetized by last name.
   • Desktop: 4-column grid.
   • Narrow screens: 2 columns.
   • Small mobile screens: 1 column.
   • Popover has a maximum height and scrolls internally when needed.
   • Clicking names does not select or remove students.
   • Click outside or press Escape to close.

Data safety
-----------
No roster, Participation, Timeliness, reminder, Classroom, grading-term,
Student Selector pool, theme, or shell storage schemas were changed.
