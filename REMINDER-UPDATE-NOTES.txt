Teacher Dashboard 4.0 — Reminder Utility Update

Changes in this build:
1. Student autocomplete searches all active period rosters, regardless of the reminder timing period.
   - Suggestions show the student's class underneath the name.
   - The selected Period controls only when the reminder fires.
   - Stable student identity is retained even when the student comes from a different period.
2. Replaced the 10-minutes-before-class-ends creation option with Custom Reminder.
   - Choose 1–60 minutes before class ends.
   - Previously saved 10-minute reminders remain supported.
3. Restored Reminder Privacy:
   - Normal: alert details show immediately.
   - Private: student/reminder details stay hidden until Show is pressed.
   - Private reminder cards in the Reminder utility are also hidden until Show is pressed.
4. Restored Clear Completed.
   - Clears completed (Done) reminders only.
   - Active reminders are not affected.

Only reminders.html and shared/reminder-service.js were functionally changed.
